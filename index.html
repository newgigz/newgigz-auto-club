GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?
We've got answers.

A few important things to know before our nationwide launch.

Can dealerships from any U.S. state participate?

NEWGIGZ Auto Club is preparing to welcome licensed automobile dealerships across all 50 U.S. states. Dealerships may express interest now. Advertising eligibility is subject to applicable state requirements, business verification, dealer license verification, platform approval and launch availability.

Does NEWGIGZ Auto Club sell vehicles?

No. NEWGIGZ Auto Club is an advertising and vehicle-discovery platform. Vehicles are offered by independent dealerships. Buyers communicate and transact directly with dealerships.

Are vehicle listings available now?

Not yet. The marketplace is preparing for its nationwide launch. Public listings will appear after dealerships have completed verification and their listings are approved.

How do I know whether a dealership is verified?

Participating dealerships must undergo license and account verification before advertising. Verification does not guarantee a vehicle's condition, title, history or the dealership's performance. Buyers should conduct independent checks.

Does NEWGIGZ Auto Club handle payments or financing?

No. We do not process vehicle purchases, financing, deposits, title transfers or negotiations. These arrangements are handled directly between the buyer and the dealership.

How much does it cost for dealerships to join the program?

Selected founding dealerships may advertise up to 20 vehicles free for six months, beginning on their approved activation date and subject to verification, approval and a written agreement. At the end of the trial, dealers will be directed to paid subscription plans. They must select and authorize a plan to continue advertising. No automatic charges apply; public listings will be hidden without an active paid subscription after the trial.
